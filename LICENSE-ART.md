## What counts as artwork

- `art/source/` — the original character sheets and the wordmark image
- `art/build/final/` — the traced vector versions of them
- `plugin/hooks/art/` — the drawings the plugin shows (generated from the above)
- `docs/images/` — screenshots and stills

Everything else (the plugin's code, the art tools' scripts, the documents) is under the MIT
license in `LICENSE`.

## In plain words

- **You may** download, use, share, change and build on the artwork: new outfits, new poses,
  new animations, other themes made from it.
- **You may not** sell it, or use it in anything you sell or earn money from.
- **Please credit** "claudia-theme by DavidZeng-creator and contributors" with a link to this
  repository when you share it or something made from it, and say if you changed it.

The original images were generated with AI tools. Whether, and how far, AI-generated images
can be protected by copyright differs from country to country; this license states how the
project asks everyone to treat the artwork. Contributions of artwork to this repository are
accepted under the same license.
