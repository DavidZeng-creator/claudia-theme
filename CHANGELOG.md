## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-20

The first public release.

- **Opening** in each new session: the wordmark assembling on an open book, fitted to the
  chat area, then her entrance in a bloom of light and petals.
- **Background**: the orange-haired librarian behind the conversation, on parchment (velvet
  in dark mode), with the theme's orange, cream and gold palette across the app.
- **Two outfits**, the white dress and the maid outfit, switched with a flash of light and
  petals (menu, right-click, a click on bare background, or `/claudia dress|maid`).
- **The ✿ flower menu** above the prompt, in one line: outfit, clarity, replay the opening,
  calibrate, and hide or show the whole look.
- **Follows the window**: any change of the window's width hides her at once; once still,
  she returns beside the right sidebar with her entrance. The session rail hiding when the
  window leaves full screen is recognised.
- **One-click calibration** of the session list's width, with a setting as fallback.
- **Hide background** gives the app its own look back, keeping only the flower; the choice is
  remembered across sessions.
- **Art tools** to rebuild every drawing from the original images.
